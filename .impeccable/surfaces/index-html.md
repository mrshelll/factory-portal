## Scope

The whole portal (sign-in, «Tus proyectos», a project's page, the piece frame, the owner's views), web,
320–1440 px, light and dark. Visitor mode: Operate.

## Audience and task

Invited, often non-technical people answering what the factory asked of them; the project owner
following who opened and who sent. Task: see what waits for you, open it, answer, know it arrived.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portal is the loom that stops: one thread runs through what was asked of you and breaks
exactly where the factory waits for a person. It refuses the category-default grey list with one
accent and status pills as the only character.

OWN-WORLD: Aizome indigo and undyed cotton. Indigo shell (top bar) #1F2A4E with muted text #B6BED9 and the
亻 stroke in light violet #A8A2FF; white cotton work area; indigo-tinted neutrals (ink #151B30, muted
#535B78, lines #E4E6F0/#C9CDDD); thread #2E3B6E; violet #4137FF only for actions and the person's stop
(tint #F3F2FF); state pills: in progress violet on #E4E1FF, not started indigo-grey, sent green #17663B on
#E6F4EC. One family, Figtree. Hairline panels, small radii, no texture, no fabric imagery.

STORY: The visitor sees at once which item waits for them (the cut thread with the 亻 disc), opens it,
answers, and sees it sit on a whole thread as «Enviada». The owner reads state, never drafts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Indigo top bar (logo + «ninben» left, «Entraste como …» and «Salir» right); the way back,
the project title and the owner-sees line on white; «Lo que se te pidió» as a list whose left edge carries
the thread; the waiting row tinted with the 亻 disc in the gap of the thread; «Decisiones» below as a panel
with standard radio choices and a violet primary «Enviar respuesta».

FORM: Third of the safer register's grounded candidates (familiar end), chosen by the owner over the
canon, «Sello de recibido», «Trazo de persona» and a thread+stamp fusion. seed:fd0ef84b654c4ba6.
Approved sketch: .impeccable/mocks/decision/grounded-hilo.html and grounded-hilo-movil.html.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction

The thread: drawn only along lists of items asked of the signed-in person; it is cut, with knots and the
violet 亻 disc, at each item that waits for that person; sent and received items sit on the whole
thread. Motion: none beyond standard hover/focus; respects reduced motion.

## Unresolved

Dark-mode values of the new palette (derived by the builder, AA). Whether the owner's progress list
carries the thread (default: no; it is about other people).
